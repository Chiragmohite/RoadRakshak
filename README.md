# 🛡️ RoadRakshak AI

### AI-Powered Road Hazard Detection, Reporting & Civic Response

> **See it. Report it. Fix it.**

RoadRakshak is an AI-powered civic infrastructure platform that helps citizens report road hazards and enables municipal authorities to detect, prioritize, assign, and verify road repairs.

The platform combines computer vision, explainable severity scoring, GPS/location information, duplicate detection, priority routing, and repair verification into one complete workflow.

# 🚨 Problem 

Road damage often goes unreported, unprioritized, and unverified.

Citizens usually lack a simple way to report potholes and other road defects with proper evidence. Municipal authorities receive scattered complaints without reliable information about:

- Severity of the damage
- Exact location
- Visual evidence
- Priority
- Duplicate complaints
- Repair completion

This results in slower response times, inefficient resource allocation, and reduced public accountability.
